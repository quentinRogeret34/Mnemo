# Returns and refunds

> Demo document. Fictional content written to show the tool.

## Return window

A customer can return an item within **30 days of delivery**. The return request is opened from the order page; past the window, the button disappears and support handles exceptions by hand.

Items marked as final sale cannot be returned.

## Refunds

A returned item is refunded **within 14 days** of the warehouse receiving it, on the original payment method only.

The refund is the price the customer actually paid for the item: when the order had a discount, the refund takes it into account.

## Shipping costs

Return shipping is free for gold customers. Other customers pay a flat €5, taken from the refund.
